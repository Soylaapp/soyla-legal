Delete your Soyla account

You can delete your Soyla account and its data at any time.

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open Soyla and sign in.
  2. Tap the Me tab (the smiley face).
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete my account, then confirm.

Your account is removed immediately.

Option 2: by email

If you can no longer open the app, email soylaapp@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Soyla account". We will delete your account within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your profile (name, languages, level, city, interests, free times, year of birth, photo), your messages, session invitations, mock exam bookings and ratings, and your sign-in details are permanently deleted and cannot be restored.

What may be kept

Safety records, such as reports about misuse, may be kept for up to 90 days where needed to protect other users, and are then deleted.

See also our Privacy Policy.
