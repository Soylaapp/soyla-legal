Soyla Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Soyla is a language exchange app for adults. It helps people find speaking partners and book mock speaking exams. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Soyla is operated by the Soyla team ("we"). You can reach us at soylaapp@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password (stored securely by our hosting provider; we never see your password).
  • Profile details you give us: name, languages, level, city, interests, free times, sticker, and year of birth (used only to confirm you are 18 or older).
  • Activity in the app: messages, session invitations, mock exam bookings, ratings, blocks and reports.
  • Safety information: messages our filter blocks, and verification requests.

How we use it

  • To run the app: matching you with partners, delivering messages, and booking sessions and mock exams.
  • To keep Soyla safe and educational: reviewing reports, blocked messages and verification requests.
  • To improve the app and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not show ads.

Who can see your information

  • Other users see your name, sticker, city, languages, level, interests, verification badge and ratings. They see your messages only if you send them.
  • Moderators and administrators can see reports, blocked messages and verification requests so they can protect users.
  • Our hosting provider, Supabase, stores the data for us (servers in Ireland, EU). If we add video calls or payments, those providers will process the data needed to provide them, and we will update this policy.
  • We may share information if the law requires it or to prevent serious harm.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is open. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Me, then Delete my account. This permanently removes your profile, messages, sessions and bookings. Some safety records, such as reports about misuse, may be kept for a limited time where needed to protect others. You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data or for its deletion.

Adults only

Soyla is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor is using Soyla, contact us and we will remove the account.

Meeting in person

In-person meetings are arranged between users. We encourage meeting only in public places, for study purposes, and telling someone you trust. We cannot guarantee another user's behavior.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so users can only see what they are meant to see. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell you in the app for important changes.
